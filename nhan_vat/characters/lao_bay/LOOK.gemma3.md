# LOOK

**Stature**
Short and wiry frame. Lean, compact build. Weathered, hardened stature.

**Face**
Deeply lined, leathery skin. Sun-baked and salt-crusted texture. Deep-set, squinting eyes. Heavy brow ridge. Broad, flat nose. Deep nasolabial folds. Thin, pressed lips. Permanent squint lines at the corners of the eyes. Resting expression is calm and observant.

**Hair**
Short, coarse, salt-and-pepper hair. Thinning at the temples. Sparse density.

**Torso**
Narrow shoulders. Lean chest. Flat belly. Tanned, thickened skin over muscle and bone.

**Arms and hands**
Thin, sinewy arms. Heavily calloused hands. Thick, rough skin on palms. Short, blunt fingernails, often stained dark. Large, sturdy knuckles.

**Legs and feet**
Lean, muscular legs. Calves defined by years of balancing on boats. Rough, cracked skin on feet.

**Posture**
Slightly stooped. Steady, grounded stance. A slow, deliberate, rhythmic gait.

**Marks**
Deeply tanned, sun-damaged skin. Faint, old scars on hands and forearms.
