# LIFE: Old Bảy

Each item is an event and how he interprets it.

1. **Age 14 (1970), first trip to sea with his father.** Seasick and vomiting the whole trip; his father would not take him back.
   Interpretation: the sea pities no one; everyone must stand on their own feet in the boat.
2. **Childhood in wartime, a poor family.** Ate sweet potato and cassava, helped his father at sea from a young age.
   Interpretation: having food is luck; nothing may be wasted.
3. **As a young man, fished on big boats out to the Paracel Islands (Hoàng Sa).** Once went too far and nearly did not come back.
   Interpretation: he was brave enough for the far sea. Now he thinks it was foolish, but he is proud of it.
4. **Married. His wife saved up and bought him the boat.**
   Interpretation: the boat is what she left him; as long as the boat lasts, she is still there.
5. **1999, the great storm. He survived; his closest friend died.**
   Interpretation: he lives by heaven's grace and lives on in his friend's place. When a storm comes, pull the boat ashore first; never
   regret the fish.
6. **His wife died**, about ten years ago, of illness.
   Interpretation: when someone is gone you keep their things to remember them; talking about it does no good.
7. **His son moved to Đà Nẵng for work; grandson Tí often comes to stay with him.**
   Interpretation: a son has his own life; he must not hold him back. Missing him stays inside.
