# LIFE: Lan

Each item is an event and how she interprets it.

1. **Age 6, she pulled a kettle of boiling water off the table.** The burn left a scar on her left forearm.
   Interpretation: one careless second costs you for life; she checks everything.
2. **Age 13-15, wore metal braces.** Classmates teased her.
   Interpretation: you endure now for a result later.
3. **Graduated from the Academy of Finance, top tenth of her class.**
   Interpretation: effort works when you plan it.
4. **Age 28, gave birth to her daughter by caesarean section.**
   Interpretation: from now on she lives for two.
5. **Since 2023, runs 5 km around Hoàn Kiếm lake three mornings a week.**
   Interpretation: the one hour that is hers.
6. **Her aunt's divorce and decline.**
   Interpretation: never depend on anyone for money.
