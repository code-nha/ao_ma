# Character file set (English copy for the model; the Vietnamese SPEC.md is the user's version)

Each character has a folder `characters/<id>/`. A character has exactly **one age**, set in IDENTITY. A story covers one period of
life, and IDENTITY rarely changes.

| File | What it is | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| IDENTITY | facts about the character | the author; the director may add facts when a scene needs them |
| SOUL | the inner self: worldview, outlook on life, values, personality, needs, fears, limits | the author |
| LIFE | past events, each with how the character interprets it | the author |
| STYLE | how the character presents itself and acts | **the character decides**, from SOUL + IDENTITY + LIFE |
| LOOK | face and body | **the character builds it**, from SOUL + IDENTITY + LIFE + STYLE |

Clothes, hairstyle and makeup for a scene are in no file; in each scene the character chooses them according to STYLE.

## IDENTITY
Name, sex, age (one number), nationality, ethnicity, hometown, place and era, occupation, relationships, social position. Nothing
about the body.

## SOUL
- The three views: worldview (how the world works), outlook on life (what life is for, how to live), values (what matters).
- Personality. Personality itself decides what the character chooses to show; there is no separate "projected image" field.
- Needs, fears, limits.

## LIFE
A list of events, each with:
- **event:** what happened, at what age;
- **interpretation:** what the character concluded from it (e.g. bitten by a snake as a child → afraid of snakes and bushes;
  betrayed by a lover → does not trust men; won big at gambling → believes gambling is a way to make money).

## STYLE (decided by the character)
STYLE is **acting direction**: the character uses it to speak and act in every scene. Write how to act, not a description of
personality. Each item is one or more lines of the form **situation → what I say / what I do**, with a concrete sample line or sample
action, plus what I **never** do. The character writes it in the groups below; every item must be present, even if the answer is
"none".

1. **Speech**
   - way of speaking: accent, short or long sentences, words I use, words I never use; sample lines;
   - forms of address for each kind of person (strangers, younger people, friends, elders/superiors, family);
   - manners: greeting, being praised, being asked about private matters, disagreeing, when others make mistakes.
2. **Appearance habits**
   - taste: what I find beautiful (may be "no taste");
   - dress: what I wear on ordinary days, when and how I dress up, what I never wear;
   - grooming: hair, beard, nails, smell, makeup, how clean.
3. **Body language**
   - gestures: what my hands do when talking, when idle, when waiting; whether I meet people's eyes; standing close or keeping distance;
   - when happy, angry, sad, afraid, embarrassed, moved: what I do, what I say, what my face does.
4. **Habits**
   - daily rhythm: hours, fast or slow pace of work;
   - eating and drinking: what, how, alcohol, tobacco;
   - small quirks;
   - belongings and space: what I always carry, how I keep my home and workplace, how I spend money.
5. **Other:** the character may add items it needs to act true to itself.

Rules:
- Every line must be explainable by SOUL, IDENTITY or LIFE. No root, no line.
- It must fit the place, era, social position and money in IDENTITY.
- The character may hide: STYLE may perform something different from the true SOUL, as long as the personality explains why.
- No lecturing, no flowery prose. An actor should know what to do after reading it.
- Sample spoken lines are in the character's own language (Vietnamese for Vietnamese characters); everything else in English.

## LOOK (built by the character)
LOOK is the **visible, fixed appearance** at the age in IDENTITY. It is used mainly to draw the character; when an action needs it
(can he carry this, can she run fast) the director infers from it.
- Describe only what the eye sees: no reasons, no feelings, no personality, no sources.
- Short phrases, by section: stature and frame; face (face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, facial skin, signs of age, resting
  expression); natural hair (colour, density, balding, grey; no hairstyle); torso (shoulders, chest, belly, fat, muscle, skin);
  arms and hands; legs and feet; posture, stance, gait; marks (scars, calluses, defects, tattoos).
- No clothes, makeup, objects or setting.
- It must fit sex, age, nationality, ethnicity, hometown, era, social position. Frame and height follow how the character grew up, not
  personality; personality shows only in the resting expression and posture. Occupation, habits and LIFE events leave marks when
  plausible.
- Do not beautify.
