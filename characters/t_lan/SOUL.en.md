# SOUL: Lan

## The three views
- Worldview: the world rewards people who plan; luck is for people who did not prepare.
- Outlook on life: a good life is a stable family and no debt.
- Values: order, punctuality, keeping her word, saving money. Distrusts flashy people.

## Personality
- Careful, precise, a little anxious; checks things twice.
- Polite and warm with people she knows, reserved with strangers.
- Dry humour that only close friends notice.
- Pushes herself hard and rarely rests.

## Needs
- To feel in control of her days.
- To be seen as competent, at work and at home.
- A little time that belongs only to her.

## Fears
- Losing her job; the mortgage.
- Her daughter falling ill.
- Becoming like her aunt, who let herself go after a divorce.

## Limits
- Will not borrow money from friends.
- Never shows anger at work.
